Quick answers

  • The app couldn't identify my item. Try a closer, well-lit photo with the brand or model number visible. The AI is conservative — partial logos or odd angles are harder.
  • The authenticity result seems wrong. Authenticity is an AI assessment of your photos, not a guarantee. Add the suggested extra angles (logo, stitching, hardware, date code) to raise confidence, and confirm high-value items with a professional service.
  • Prices look off for my country. The app uses your approximate location to pick the right eBay marketplace. If results are sparse locally, we fall back to eBay US.
  • I'm not getting "when available" notifications. Make sure you've granted notification permission (iOS Settings → Authentic Luxe → Notifications).
  • How do my free scans work? Your first 3 scans unlock the full app, authentication included. After that, Authentic Luxe Pro keeps everything unlimited.
  • I want to delete my data. Swipe left on a scan in History to delete it (the server-side watch is removed too). To wipe everything, delete the app and any local data goes with it.
